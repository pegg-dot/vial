# VialGrade: legal cross-reference and code-evidence matrix

**Review date: October 16, 2026. Status: OPEN, NOT LEGAL CERTIFICATION.**
This file supplements [COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_2026-10-16.md](COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_2026-10-16.md) in draft PR #22. It matches U.S. primary agency/statutory sources to actual VialGrade code. No internet survey or software review can establish compliance with *every* federal, state, local, and foreign law. Compliance depends on actual visitors, operators, vendors, claims, payments, contracts and evolving law. **Do not merge, deploy or represent the site as “completely legal” on the basis of this file.**

## Severity rubric
- **Stop / legal gate**: material facts or legally necessary controls are unverified; requires counsel and product-owner action before exposing the implicated feature.
- **Partially mitigated**: source and code checked, technical risk reduced; ongoing audits, historical data, or human evidence needed.
- **Conditional**: applies only when specified activity, geography or business threshold is actually present. Not a blanket finding of applicability.
- **Editorial**: remaining false or poorly substantiated text must be reviewed.

## What is actually implemented in PR #22

- Public checkout and former seller redirects are paused by a central route list and a backend outbound-helper guard. They are **not** general legal permission to refer customers to unapproved drugs.
- Existing authenticated financial remediation is left operational; prior blanket blockage was removed when HTTP role tests exposed it.
- FDA status is substance-specific; unapproved research products are not declared lawful because of a disclaimer, and retatrutide/cagrilintide restrictions are separately noted.
- Vendor grade, purity, review, and historical price claims have some safer wording, but the evidence underlying **each row** is not independently substantiated by these code changes.
- Analytics replaces specific health-interest paths with coarse categories going forward; automated retention window is shorter. Older rows, backups and account saved-search histories remain in scope.
- Terms, privacy and marketing pages were revised; they are not guarantees that live operations, provider arrangements, consumer rights or effective consents are correct.

## Release blockers ranked by practical impact

1. **Regulated human-use marketing / claim-by-claim review.** FDA/FTC counsel must review every public entry point (search, product, compound, review, news, metadata and social preview). Remove unsubstantiated claims. Decide whether the business is documentary evidence research or an actual vendor referral service; do not blend them.
2. **WA and any other applicable health-data jurisdiction.** Determine where users are located/targeted and whether named compound saves/preferences are covered sensitive health data. Implement jurisdictionally sufficient notice, collection consent/necessity, separate sharing consent, withdrawal, access/deletion and dedicated policy if required, or stop the feature in covered jurisdictions.
3. **Named vendor evidence and adverse grades.** Attach original-source URLs, observed dates, lab-issued samples, chain-of-custody limitations and a reviewer/corrections trail before publishing a negative accusation.
4. **Copyright and redistribution rights** for lab certificates, vendor imagery, third-party reviews and scraper content.
5. **Verified legal operator and service-provider contracts.** Establish who is actually publishing, which laws and tax rules apply, what Vercel/Neon and other processors retain, whether email inboxes function, and accuracy of venue/terms.
6. **Historical privacy data.** Review/delete former detailed browsing paths and affiliate click records consistent with lawful retention, legal holds and real backup procedures; verify automated cleanup actually runs.
7. **End-to-end technical test and preview audit.** No merge until lint/typecheck/integration/security/accessibility/HTTP role audit/Playwright are green against the **final SHA** and preview has been clicked through.
8. **Actual regulatory attorney sign-off** identifying assumed jurisdictions, applicable laws, factual limitations and permissible business/monetization scope.

**Known unknowns:** ownership, age and location of actual customers, future commissions, uploaded documents' copyright terms, exact source verification outcomes, processing in backups, operational legal notices, all 50 states' current requirements, and foreign targeting. None can be safely guessed away.

**Release decision:** Remain in draft. A technically passing preview can still be unlawful. Only merge following owner decision, verified scope and competent legal review.
