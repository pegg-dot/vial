## Research and commerce safety

- Only human-reviewed starter scientific identities and editorial source references drive the new public market. There is **no** import of old merchant price, inventory, old buyer behavior, certificates or reputation data.
- Public sales endpoints, pricing referrals and old /market remain quarantined: direct checkout HTTP 410 and old market route redirects to retired notice.
- File A/B/C grades indicate which 1-3 curated source layers are linked, **not** efficacy, physical purity, scientific consensus, lab certification, or recommendation.
- The external Vercel AI Gateway panel remains localhost-only / credential-gated; no provider call was made for this milestone. The model is not a biomedical evidence source.
- Historical private customer account/privacy rights are preserved by existing server guards and are not repurposed for research discovery.
- Source coverage dates and the exact Pexiganan study-to-article match still require independent scientific review; public research is not a formal systematic literature review.
