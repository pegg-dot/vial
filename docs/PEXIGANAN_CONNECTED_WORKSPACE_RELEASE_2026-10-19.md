# VialGrade Pexiganan source-workspace release

October 19, 2026. Status: local implementation pending release CI and production checks.

## Goal

Preserve the original VialGrade market and visual identity, while helping researchers and biotech diligence analysts distinguish molecular identity, laboratory experiments, registered studies, secondary reviews, and unverified physical-material records. Do not claim peptide efficacy, medical use, purchasable supplies, regulatory permission or independently authenticated physical lots.

## Scope

Four selectable evidence questions appear directly under the Pexiganan dossier identity. Selecting a particular PubChem, PubMed or ClinicalTrials.gov record updates an in-context inspector with its attributed observation, limitations and original link. On narrow screens source documents form a scrollable row, followed by their inspector. The prior dossier, six claim evidence passport, registry audits, source verifier, material checker, global search, laboratory/company data and comparison flows remain reachable.

The research file now emphasizes the count of linked source layers rather than a large letter grade, without changing the underlying A/B/C method. The decorative dossier illustration occupies less vertical space on mobile. Existing public editorial source metadata is reused; no AI calls, new scientific deductions, seller information or drug-commerce capabilities were added.

## Explicit pivot and roadmap

Old sequence: accumulate distinct evidence pages, then explain how to navigate them. New sequence: give Pexiganan one cohesive exact-source investigation, test that with real researchers, then extend the pattern to the rest of the research library. This advances evidence reconciliation, not a superficial stylistic change. Current: full checks and gated GitHub/Vercel release. Next: validate source links and checkout HTTP 410 on the production domain. Parked: scientific efficacy rankings, sales, vendor endorsements, new data ingestion and paid AI. Open: human scientific peer review, licensing, legal compliance, researcher comprehension and authentication of any future physical lots.

## Release safety

Production and preview must set VIALGRADE_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_ONLY=true, preserve protected historical account access and deny retired purchase/partner endpoints. A source-file letter reports editorial documentation coverage, not outcomes, safety, purity or supplier quality. The source snapshot is editorial and dated; original sources must be inspected before real-world scientific decisions.

## Verification

Record final tests, GitHub SHA, Vercel deployment and actual live HTTP checks only after observed success. Do not describe an unverified deployment as complete.
