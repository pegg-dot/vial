# VIAL 0.7 — Complete Internal Marketplace

VIAL 0.7 adds the remaining sandbox-operable layers that do not require external providers: identities and roles, seller catalog and teams, durable notification records, support cases, review moderation, versioned policy simulation, fraud cases, synthetic analytics and feature flags, agent evaluation gates, privacy requests, and executable scenario simulations.

All records and companies remain fictional. Production authentication, messaging, money movement, tax, carrier, identity-verification, and legal approvals remain disabled.
