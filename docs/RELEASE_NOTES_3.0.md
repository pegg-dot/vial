# VIAL 3.0 release notes

VIAL 3.0 adds the consumer intelligence layer on top of the V1 production foundation and V2 market-data engine.

## Added

- Personalized `/for-you` market with transparent ranking reasons
- Durable saved searches and rerun history
- Cross-device comparison sessions and named snapshots
- Explicit price, evidence, notification, and quiet-hour preferences
- Followed compound, vendor, and listing relationships
- Private decision history and activity continuity
- Deterministic “since your last visit” summaries
- Relevance-ranked in-product notifications
- Database-backed watchlists and mobile retention navigation
- Consumer intelligence APIs and staff aggregate dashboard
- Tenant-isolation, quiet-hour, ranking, and persistence tests

## Preserved boundaries

- All vendors, products, reports, accounts, and transactions remain fictional
- No medical recommendations, dosing guidance, injection guidance, or physical-vial claims
- Personalization uses explicit market preferences rather than inferred health goals
- Production commerce remains disabled
